Our Story

Nurturing a love for reading and community since 2015.

Est. 2015

A Haven for Book Lovers

What started as a small corner bookshop has grown into the city's premier reading destination. LeafShelf was founded on a simple principle: books should be accessible, and reading should be a shared experience.

Over the years, we've expanded our collection to over 50,000 titles, spanning every genre imaginable. But we remain true to our roots—a cozy, welcoming space where anyone can lose themselves in a good book.

Sarah Jenkins
Founder & Head Librarian

Our Mission

To curate exceptional literature and foster a vibrant, inclusive community where the joy of reading connects and inspires individuals.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and beloved cultural hub for readers worldwide, blending the charm of traditional libraries with modern accessibility.
